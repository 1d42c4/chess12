CLEAR SCOPE, CHECKABLE ANSWERS

What this course can claim.

A complete study route through its stated practical foundations, with exercises and a review method. It is not an encyclopedia of every chess position or a promise of a particular rating.

Original teaching material

The lessons and site were created with AI for knightway8’s Human Chess Project. The explanations are original instructional writing. Practice positions were generated for this collection rather than copied from another author’s annotated lessons. Human learning remains the focus: inspect, attempt, explain, and use the idea in a real game.

How this practice set was checked

The 120 tactical exercises came from original simulated legal games. Stockfish 19 analyzed each selected position with MultiPV 3 and a 200,000-node search. The selected first move had a substantial score gap in that recorded search. This is finite engine analysis, not an exhaustive proof of uniqueness; another legal move may still be playable. The stored principal variation is one continuation, not a claim that every reply is forced.

References and tools

  • FIDE Laws of Chess: authoritative rules for legal moves and over-the-board play. Tournament and platform procedures can differ.
  • chess.js 1.4.0: legal move generation, attack maps, and board state. Its BSD-2-Clause license is included in the repository.
  • Stockfish: Stockfish 19 was used locally to generate and analyze the tactical practice set. The engine executable is not needed by this website and is not bundled.
  • Lichess tablebase server and API documentation: exact small-piece endgame results used in the endgame course.
  • Lichess Practice: optional further practice with an independent interface. This course does not copy its lesson prose.

Board artwork

Colin M. L. Burnett’s Cburnett vector pieces are distributed unmodified from Lichess under GPL version 2 or later. The clear outlines, cream and olive squares, and outside coordinates follow the supplied design reference. Artwork credits and source · Full license.

Read the limits correctly

A legal position is not necessarily a strategically sensible one. Some exercises isolate elementary skills in unusual positions. A count or engine answer does not replace an explanation of the mechanism. The course distinguishes rule-based counts, finite engine analysis, and exact tablebase results rather than presenting them as the same kind of evidence.

Report a correction

Open an issue in this repository with the lesson or exercise ID, the position or FEN, the claimed problem, and a proposed correction or line. Avoid personal information in public issues. Updates should use pull requests so the protected branch keeps its history.

Browser storage

Progress stays under a separate key for this course. There are no analytics, remote fonts, or external scripts. If browser storage is unavailable, use the export control to keep the current session’s record.
